How it works

Three steps, from your door to a fresh pile of laundry.

  1. 1

    Choose a shop

    Browse shops in Balete. Compare services, rates and what each shop offers: drop-off, pickup or delivery.

  2. 2

    Send a booking request

    Pick a service, estimate your weight and choose a date and time. When the shop accepts, your Order ID is created.

  3. 3

    Track it and collect

    Watch your order move from Accepted to Ready. You pay the shop directly when you drop off, pick up or receive delivery.

Made for everyday laundry

What you can do in Drop, Wash, Go today.

  • {{ icon("bag") }}

    Drop-off, pickup or delivery

    Choose how your laundry gets to the shop and back. Shops show which options they offer.

  • {{ icon("clipboard") }}

    Clear prices

    See each shop's rates and delivery fee before you book. The final price is set when your laundry is weighed.

  • {{ icon("grid") }}

    Order tracking

    A simple timeline shows where your order is, from Pending to Completed.

  • {{ icon("bell") }}

    Notifications

    Get told when a shop accepts your booking, starts washing, or has your laundry ready.

  • {{ icon("calendar") }}

    Pick your time

    Choose a date and a time slot. Bookings outside a shop's opening hours are not allowed.

  • {{ icon("user") }}

    Safe accounts

    Reset a forgotten password with a security question. Each shop only sees its own orders.
